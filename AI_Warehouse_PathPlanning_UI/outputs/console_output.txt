Why Manhattan distance is a lower bound on the remaining travel cost:
  * Each move changes the row OR the column by exactly 1, so reaching G needs at
    least |dr| + |dc| moves, whatever obstacles are in the way.
  * Every move costs at least 1 (open = 1, crowded = 4).
  => remaining cost >= number of moves >= Manhattan distance, so h never
     overestimates (admissible). It is also consistent: h(n) <= c(n,n') + h(n').
